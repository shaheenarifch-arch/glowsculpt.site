Beauty Technology Guide

At-home beauty technology covers several categories, including powered cleansing, microcurrent-style toning devices and light-based skincare tools. These are different technologies and should not be treated as interchangeable. Compare the intended use, required companion products, session length, charging method, app requirements and manufacturer instructions.

Before purchasing, check what is included in the box, the warranty offered by the merchant or manufacturer, replacement-part requirements and the return policy. For any device making performance claims, rely on the manufacturer instructions and appropriate professional advice rather than social-media claims.

Shopping checklist

  • Confirm the exact product and included accessories.
  • Read manufacturer directions and warnings.
  • Check price, delivery and returns on the merchant site.
  • Compare products by routine fit rather than marketing language.
