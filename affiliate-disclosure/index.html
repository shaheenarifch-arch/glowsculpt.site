How affiliate links work

Affiliate links identify the referring publisher when a visitor moves from GlowSculpt to a merchant website. Product information can change after the feed is published, so customers should verify important details on the merchant website before payment.

Affiliate eligibility does not guarantee inclusion. The product edit prioritises relevance, distinct imagery, useful category coverage and product information available from the feed. Commercial links are marked for search engines using sponsored and nofollow relationship attributes.
